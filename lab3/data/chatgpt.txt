ChatGPT is an artificial intelligence chatbot developed by OpenAI. It can understand and generate human-like text based on the questions or instructions given by users. ChatGPT can help with tasks such as answering questions, writing content, explaining concepts, generating code, summarizing information, and learning new topics. It uses advanced language models to understand context and provide useful responses. ChatGPT is widely used by students, developers, professionals, and businesses for education, productivity, and creative work.
